## Technical substance identified

The submission makes five targeted requests for clarification: dynamic enforcement of collection scope; third-party provenance and processing responsibilities; verification of minimisation and exclusion safeguards; change-triggered reassessment of existing decisions; and evidence of safeguards and effective intervention.

One concrete implementation example is a versioned acquisition-control record linking a material collection run to purpose, source-selection criteria and exclusions, crawler configuration, detected boundary changes, and accountable disposition. The submission describes illustrative evidence such as run identifiers, policy versions, filter configuration and test results, exceptions, review responsibility, and release, quarantine, recollection, or exclusion decisions. It cautions that granularity should be proportionate and that linked manifests may avoid retaining raw content.

The submission explicitly treats these proposed fields as illustrative examples, not universal technical mandates or determinations of GDPR lawfulness.
