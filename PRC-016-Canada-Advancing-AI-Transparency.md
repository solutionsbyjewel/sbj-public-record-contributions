# PRC-016 — Canada: Advancing AI Transparency

> **Private review draft. Not approved for public release.** This SBJ-authored summary describes recommendations in SBJ’s contribution; it is not an ISED, Canadian government, or regulator position or endorsement.

**Contribution reviewed:** Solutions by Jewel, *Advancing AI Transparency in Canada: AI Agents, AI Governance and Incident Transparency*. The SBJ-hosted public URL has not yet been verified for this draft and must be added before any public release.

## Technical substance identified

For consequential agent actions, the submission says records should be capable of connecting the action to:

- the agent or execution identity;
- the human, system, organization, or other source of authority;
- the applicable authority scope and material conditions;
- tools, systems, or resources used;
- material delegation or agent-to-agent handoffs;
- required human approval or intervention;
- the consequential action or resulting state change; and
- whether authority remained valid when the action occurred.

It also describes proportionate information needs for enterprise users, including system/version, functions and limitations, tools and integrations, data/resource access, delegation, intervention, evidence preservation, dependencies, authority-changing conditions, termination, and recovery/resumption behavior.
