# PRC-014 — UK NPL: AI Testing, Evaluation and Assurance

> **Private review draft. Not approved for public release.** This SBJ-authored summary describes recommendations in SBJ’s contribution; it is not an NPL position, laboratory result, or endorsement.

**Contribution reviewed:** Solutions by Jewel, response to the UK Call for Evidence on AI Testing, Evaluation and Assurance. The SBJ-hosted public URL has not yet been verified for this draft and must be added before any public release.

## Technical substance identified

The response identifies three governance and reporting gaps for assurance:

1. **Validity over time:** state the conditions and changes under which an evaluation result remains applicable or requires reassessment.
2. **Evaluated-object definition:** identify the system and execution context represented by a result, including relevant model/version, configuration, tools, dependencies, credentials, and environment for agentic systems.
3. **Evidence sufficiency:** report the evidence and access level supporting a determination, including where evidence is insufficient, rather than presenting results without their basis.

The response distinguishes these governance/reporting questions from laboratory measurement, model benchmarking, datasets, and model access, which it says are outside SBJ’s scope.

## Gate assessment

**Provisional 4/5 — selective candidate.** These are reusable assurance-reporting requirements with concrete categories of evidence and system context. They align with SBJ’s architecture work while applying it specifically to the validity and reproducibility of assurance results.
